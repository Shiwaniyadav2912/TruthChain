How it works

Three steps, one guarantee

The order matters — hashing happens the moment a record is created, storage happens once, and verification can happen forever after.

01 / HASH

Every record gets a fingerprint

On publish, the exact bytes of the article or photo run through SHA-256, producing a fixed 64-character fingerprint unique to that content.

02 / STORE

The fingerprint is locked in

The hash, publisher name, and timestamp are written to the ledger. Nothing about the original content is stored — only its fingerprint.

03 / VERIFY

Anyone can re-check it

Paste the same content back in and TruthChain re-hashes it live. One changed character produces a completely different fingerprint.
